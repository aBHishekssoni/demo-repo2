
# Prevention of Sexual Harassment


## What behaviour counts as sexual harassment?

- Unwelcome physical contact or advances
- Demand or request for sexual favours
- Sexually coloured remarks, jokes, or comments on looks
- Showing pornography or sharing inappropriate messages or images
- Staring, stalking, or repeated unwanted attention
- Any other unwelcome verbal, non-verbal, or physical conduct of a sexual nature
- Threats or promises tied to work (promotion, job) in exchange for favours

## What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

- **Say no clearly** to the person, if it is safe to do so
- **Keep records:** dates, times, messages, witnesses
- **Report it** to the Internal Committee (IC), HR, or a senior
- **File a written complaint** (within 3 months of the incident, extendable by another 3 months)
- **Support the victim** if I am a witness, and report what I saw
- **Keep it confidential** and avoid gossip
- **Report repeated incidents** each time, with the records
